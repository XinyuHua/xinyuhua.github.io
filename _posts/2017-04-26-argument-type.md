title: A Study On The Diversity Of Supporting Argument in Online Debate
---

This is the work we submitted to ACL 2017. We study the way human debaters pick and combine different kinds of information
from online documents to form a persuasive and coherent argument.
Usually gathering relevant documents and potential references is not hard, given the power of the Internet. 
But choosing the most related and synthesizing those arguments together can be very challenging.
We want to classify different kinds of arguments and see how people choose among them given different context.
